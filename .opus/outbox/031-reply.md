## What shipped

- `src/components/cursor/particles.tsx` — `<Particles />` R3F component built on a `BufferGeometry` of 3000 points with two custom attributes (`aTerminal`, `aEarth`). A custom `ShaderMaterial` mixes between the two attribute sets with a single `uProgress` uniform; the fragment shader makes round, additive-blended glowing dots that shift colour from `#4a9eff` (blue, terminal) to `#ffaa00` (warm, earth) as progress goes 0→1. `aEarth` positions are uniform points on the sphere of radius 2 (using the canonical `latLngToXYZ` from T009 with random lat/lng) so the reformed cloud sits directly on the wireframe earth.
- Exported helpers: `createParticleMaterial()` and `tweenProgress(material, to, duration?)` — the GSAP tween targets `material.uniforms.uProgress` (an object), **not** the particle object itself, exactly as the inbox specified.
- `ParticlesHandle` ref API: `dissolveFromTerminal()` (tween to 1) and `reformAsEarth()` (tween to 0). The handle is wired in a `useEffect`, not during render, to satisfy `react-hooks/refs`.
- `src/app/[locale]/earth-preview/page.tsx` — now mounts `<Particles handleRef={...} />` beside `<Earth />` inside the same `<Canvas>`. Adds a dev-only `keydown` listener gated by `process.env.NODE_ENV === "development"` that triggers `d` → dissolve, `r` → reform. In a prod build the listener is never registered (the early return is the first line of the effect, before `addEventListener`).
- `tests/unit/particles.test.ts` — three vitest checks: initial `uProgress = 0`, `tweenProgress(..., 1)` resolves with `uProgress ≈ 1`, and the reform path tweens back to `0`. These cover the progress→shader-uniform contract called out in the inbox.
- `specs/001-the-cursor/tasks.md` — all four T010 boxes ticked in the same product commit (Principle 10).
